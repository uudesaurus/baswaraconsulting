## The Three-Dimensional Matrix

Every page in the website connects three dimensions:

```
INDUSTRIES (The "Who")
    ↓
    ├─→ CAPABILITIES (The "What")
    │       ↓
    │       └─→ UNITS (The "How")
    │
    └─→ INSIGHTS (The "Proof")
```

### Example User Journey: Mining Industry

1. User lands on **Mining** industry page (`/industries/mining`)
2. Sees local context: "RKAB Approval & Hilirisasi"
3. Discovers related capabilities:
   - Operations & Supply Chain
   - Sustainability & ESG
   - Digital & Technology
4. Learns about delivery units:
   - Baswara X (for digital/IoT solutions)
   - Baswara Implementation (for on-ground execution)
5. Reads relevant insights:
   - "The Future of Nickel Downstreaming"
   - "Building Supply Chain Resilience"
6. Can navigate to:
   - Capability detail pages to learn methodology
   - Unit pages to understand delivery model
   - Contact page to start conversation
